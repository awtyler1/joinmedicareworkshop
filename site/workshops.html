Before you ask

Questions about attending

Is anything sold at the class?

No. This is an educational event: no specific plans discussed, no enrollment forms, no follow-up unless you request it.

What if I can't make my date?

Register anyway. We'll send the recording. The live Q&A is honestly the best part, so come live if you can.

Can my spouse or daughter join too?

Please bring them. One seat covers your household, and a second set of ears helps more than people expect.

Will I be on camera?

Never. You can see and hear us. We can't see you. Questions can be typed or asked out loud, whichever you prefer.

How long is it, really?

The teaching is about 50 minutes. The Q&A runs as long as there are questions, usually another 15 to 20. Leave whenever you need to.
