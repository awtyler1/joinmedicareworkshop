Let's be honest

Medicare isn't hard because you can't understand it. It's hard because everyone explaining it is selling something.

The average person turning 65 gets buried under dozens of mailers, robocalls, and TV ads, every single one from someone with an agenda. So we took everything we explain to clients one-on-one, cut every word of jargon, and packed it into one free hour. Here's what that hour covers:

  1. The four parts, decoded

    What A, B, C, and D actually do, with examples from real life instead of a government pamphlet.

  2. The deadlines with teeth

    Certain enrollment windows close and never reopen without a penalty, one you pay for the rest of your life. You'll leave knowing your exact dates.

  3. Advantage and Supplement, no spin

    The honest trade-offs on your doctors, your travel, and your total cost. The comparison the TV commercials aren't designed to give you.

  4. The five expensive mistakes

    Each one avoidable in minutes if someone shows you. Someone's about to show you.
