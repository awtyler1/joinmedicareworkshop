How we decide what to publish

Three tests, every time.

We're putting this in public so you can hold us to it.

True

Every fact checked against Medicare.gov, CMS, or Social Security, with the sources listed and the date we verified it shown at the top. Numbers change yearly, so anything stale gets fixed or pulled.

Useful

You should be able to do something within five minutes of finishing: check a date, make a call, fill in a line. If a piece only informs, it isn't finished yet.

Clear

Read aloud before it publishes. One idea per sentence, no jargon, and no word we wouldn't use at a kitchen table. If it needs a glossary, we simplify until it doesn't.
