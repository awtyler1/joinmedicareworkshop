Questions, answered

Before you ask —

Is it truly free?

Truly. If you ever want one-on-one help after class, insurance companies pay agents — never you — and your price is identical with or without an agent. The workshop itself is education, full stop.

Will anything be sold at this event?

No. This is an educational event: no specific plans discussed, no enrollment forms, no follow-up unless you request it.

I'm not comfortable with computers.

If you can click a link in an email, you can attend. We send simple instructions, and you're welcome to call us for help — or simply join by telephone.

I'm not 65 yet — too early?

Within a year of 65 is the ideal time. The expensive mistakes come from starting late, never from starting early.
