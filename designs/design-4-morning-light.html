Good questions

Things people usually ask first

Is this really free? What's the catch?

Really free, no catch. If you ever want one-on-one help later, insurance companies pay agents — never you — and your price is identical either way. The workshop is pure education, always.

Will you try to sell me anything?

No. This is an educational event: no specific plans discussed, no enrollment forms, and no follow-up calls unless you ask for one.

I'm not great with technology. Can I still join?

If you can click a link in an email, you're all set. We send simple instructions, we're one phone call away if you get stuck, and you can even join by phone.

I'm not 65 yet — should I wait?

Please don't! Within a year of 65 is the perfect time. The expensive mistakes come from starting too late, never too early.
