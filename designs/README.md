# The Medicare Workshop — Design Directions

Four complete landing-page design directions for the Medicare workshop site
(Tyler Insurance Group, Lexington KY). Each is a self-contained HTML file with
full real copy: hero, value stack, "why it's free" honesty section, Meet Your
Hosts, testimonials, upcoming sessions, FAQ, registration form, and
educational-event compliance disclaimers.

## The four directions

| # | Name | Personality | Best if we want to feel like… |
|---|------|-------------|-------------------------------|
| 1 | **The Front Porch** (`design-1-front-porch.html`) | Warm oat paper, bluegrass green, honey accent, serif type | A trusted neighbor writing you a letter. Maximum hometown warmth. |
| 2 | **Straight Talk** (`design-2-straight-talk.html`) | Ink on white, marker-yellow highlights, giant heavy type, value stack with dollar values | The Hormozi route: raw, disarming, offer-so-good-it-feels-dumb-to-say-no. |
| 3 | **The Study Hall** (`design-3-study-hall.html`) | Warm paper, deep ink blue, sealing-wax red, scholarly serif, numbered curriculum | A free evening course from a respected institution. Maximum credibility. |
| 4 | **Morning Light** (`design-4-morning-light.html`) | Dawn-sky blues, butter-yellow sun, rounded cards, big humanist sans | The optimistic side of retirement. Friendly, modern, approachable. |

## Shared strategy baked into every version

- **Senior-first UX**: 19–21px body text, high contrast, huge tap targets,
  literal button copy, prominent phone number (many seniors will call instead
  of filling a form), no carousels.
- **Hormozi value equation**: dream outcome (finally understand Medicare) ×
  likelihood (taught by licensed locals) ÷ time (one hour) ÷ effort (from your
  couch, free). Value-stacked free offer; risk removed via the "No-Pitch"
  promise; honest urgency from real enrollment deadlines.
- **Radical honesty disarm**: every version answers "why is it free?" with the
  real business model, in plain language.
- **Compliance**: all copy is written in the CMS *educational event* framing —
  "learn," never "enroll" — with the standard "not affiliated with the federal
  Medicare program" disclaimer and Medicare.gov/1-800-MEDICARE reference in
  every footer.
- **Built to scale**: copy references "Kentucky seniors" at the top level only;
  city names appear in swappable spots so per-city pages (Lexington,
  Louisville, …) are a find-and-replace away once we move to a framework.
